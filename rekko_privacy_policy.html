Rekko Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Rekko ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what the app does with data.

1. Your recordings stay on your device

Screen recordings, FaceCam video, microphone and app audio are processed only on your device and saved to your device storage (Movies/Rekko) or to the in-app Private Vault. We never upload your recordings. Nothing leaves your phone unless you choose to share it.

2. Permissions

  • Screen capture: only after you approve the Android consent dialog, every session.
  • Microphone: only while recording with Mic on.
  • Camera: only while FaceCam is on.
  • Display over other apps: for floating controls and FaceCam.
  • Notifications: recording controls and the optional weekly storage reminder.
  • Notification access (optional, Call Assist): only used to notice that a call is in progress so we can offer a Record button. We check only whether a notification is an ongoing call; we never read message content, store notifications or send them anywhere. You can turn it off anytime in Settings.

3. Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob and its mediation partners (AppLovin, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads, InMobi). These partners may collect device identifiers (advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for consent through Google's certified consent tool (IAB TCF) before any ad is requested. You can change your choice anytime in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Pro users see no ads.

4. Analytics and crash reports

We use Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics to count anonymous feature usage (for example, that a recording started or an export finished) and to fix crashes. These never include your recordings or their contents.

5. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive only a purchase token to unlock Pro; we never see your payment details.

6. Private Vault

Vault videos are stored in app-private storage and protected by your PIN or biometrics. The PIN is stored only as a salted hash on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes Vault contents.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

8. Recording others

Laws in many places require consent from everyone being recorded. You are responsible for following the laws that apply to you.
